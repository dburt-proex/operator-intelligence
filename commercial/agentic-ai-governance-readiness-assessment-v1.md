# Agentic AI Governance Readiness Assessment — Offer v1

Status: `DRAFT / COMMERCIAL REVIEW`  
Source system: Operator Intelligence  
Claim boundary: evidence-backed readiness assessment, **not certification**

## Buyer

Small or mid-sized AI product teams, agencies, consultants, workflow owners, and founders that are already using agents, tool-calling models, MCP, automation platforms, or custom AI workflows and need a clear picture of what is controlled, what is not, and what should be fixed first.

## Problem

Agentic systems often accumulate prompts, tools, permissions, integrations, human approvals, memory, automation, and deployment paths faster than governance evidence is created. The buyer may know the system works without being able to prove:

- what the agent is allowed to do;
- what requires human approval;
- where execution can escape intended scope;
- how decisions and changes are recorded;
- whether evidence supports the claimed readiness state;
- which controls should be implemented first.

## Offer

A bounded governance-readiness assessment that converts supplied system evidence into:

1. evidence inventory and evidence-quality findings;
2. readiness findings by governed criterion;
3. explicit unknown/blocked items where evidence is insufficient;
4. prioritized control gaps and remediation actions;
5. decision/traceability record;
6. short executive summary suitable for implementation planning.

## Delivery target

Standard lightweight assessment: **48 hours after required evidence is available**.

Delivery timing is a service target, not a guarantee when required evidence, authority, or scope is incomplete.

## Required intake

Minimum useful evidence may include:

- system or workflow description;
- agent/tool inventory;
- permission and approval model;
- relevant prompts/instruction files;
- MCP/integration inventory where applicable;
- deployment/runtime description;
- logging/audit evidence;
- change-control or repository evidence;
- incident/failure examples if available;
- stated business and risk objectives.

Sensitive material should be minimized. Secrets, credentials, private keys, and unrestricted production data are not required for the assessment.

## Deliverables

### 1. Readiness findings

For every evaluated criterion:

- finding/state;
- supporting evidence reference;
- evidence quality/limitations;
- rationale;
- risk or dependency;
- recommended next action.

### 2. Governance score/profile

Where the selected Operator Intelligence profile supports scoring, results are produced using the repository's versioned deterministic methodology.

A score is a decision aid, not a certification, legal conclusion, or guarantee of safety.

### 3. Gap register

Prioritized gaps grouped by practical action class, such as:

- authority and permissions;
- execution controls;
- change-time governance;
- runtime containment;
- evidence and auditability;
- human review;
- memory/context integrity;
- integration/tool provenance;
- incident/recovery controls.

### 4. Action roadmap

A bounded roadmap ordered by:

- risk reduction;
- dependency/unblocking power;
- implementation effort;
- reversibility;
- evidence value.

### 5. Decision receipt

A sanitized assessment receipt records the assessment/profile version, evidence references, decision state, limitations, and next gate without copying secrets or restricted raw evidence.

## Current proof boundary

The offer may accurately state that Operator Intelligence currently includes repository-native, versioned governance methodology and supporting validation infrastructure, including evidence admission controls, deterministic validation/scoring components where applicable, decision traceability, and field-reliability study tooling.

Synthetic and adversarial validation must be labeled as such.

## Claims currently withheld

Until accepted real field-study evidence supports them, do not claim:

- independently established field reliability;
- certification or accreditation;
- universal security or failure prevention;
- guaranteed regulatory compliance;
- guaranteed business outcome;
- broad customer adoption;
- production effectiveness beyond observed evidence.

## Scope exclusions

Unless separately contracted, this assessment does not include:

- penetration testing;
- legal advice;
- formal certification/audit opinion;
- production deployment;
- credential handling;
- direct infrastructure changes;
- autonomous remediation;
- signing contracts or accepting obligations for the buyer.
