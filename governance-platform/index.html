Governance controls

Uncertainty is preserved. Authority is explicit. Boundaries remain enforceable.

The system distinguishes performance from confidence, evidence coverage from observed maturity, publication from implementation, and unknown data from negative evidence.

Decision gates

ALLOW

The bounded artifact or action has sufficient evidence, authority, and control to advance to the next separate decision.

REVIEW

Qualified judgment, validation, correction, accepted limitation, or explicit approval is required.

HALT

Evidence, authority, safety, privacy, scope, dependency, publication, or traceability prevents advancement.

Non-negotiable boundaries

  • Unknown is never scored as zero.
  • Confidence never modifies performance or priority.
  • Package eligibility precedes package assignment.
  • Publication and implementation are separate authorizations.
  • Unsupported ROI, revenue, ranking, conversion, and timeline claims are prohibited.
  • Published records are superseded, never silently overwritten.
